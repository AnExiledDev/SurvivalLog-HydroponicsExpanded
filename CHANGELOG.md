# Changelog

## 0.1.3

- Each station's pots now change with what is planted: a pot in every bay for small plants, one big pot over two bays for a medium plant, and one over all four for a large plant. Corn, watermelon and other large crops grow in the middle of their pot instead of over the bay walls.
- The grow lights hang higher, so a fully grown crop fits under them: the tallest small crop in the Small, the tallest medium crop in the Medium, and corn in the Large.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.20719.

## 0.1.2

- With the Mod Manager, each station's `Materials` shows as item cards with a picker, and the station's package beside them with its pieces, weight and value.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.20151.

## 0.1.1

- A change to `GrowthBoost` now reaches plants already growing, keeping the growth they have made.

## 0.1.0

- First build: a Small and a Medium Hydroponic Station, each with its own model and icon, and a new look for the game's Hydroponic Station (Large).
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.20151.
