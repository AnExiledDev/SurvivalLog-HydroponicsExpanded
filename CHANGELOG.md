# Changelog

## 0.1.6

- The Small's and Medium's glowing light beam now starts at their own light bar too. 0.1.5 moved only the light itself, so the beam you see still came from high up.
- The Large's lights are back where they were in 0.1.4, low by the water, after 0.1.5 lifted them to the top where they lit nothing.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.20719.

## 0.1.5

- The Small and Medium can now be crafted in any of the 8 colours, like the Large: pick a colour on the recipe card and one dye is added to the materials. Each coloured package has its own icon.
- The Small's and Medium's grow light now shines from their own light bar. Before, it came from much higher up, where the Large's bar is.
- The big pots for medium and large crops sit on a smaller plate, so the water shows around them as it does around the small pots.
- A dye can no longer be one of a station's `Materials`, since picking a colour adds one.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.20719.

## 0.1.4

- The Small and Medium recipes can now be crafted. Before, they showed at the workbench but stayed locked.
- Planting level unlocks the stations: the Small at level 3, the Medium at 4 and the Large at 5. A run already past a level gets the recipe when the save loads.
- Getting the Large's recipe, by its level or by the Hydroponic Cultivation talent, unlocks the Small and Medium too.
- New settings: `UnlockLevel` for each station. `0` turns the level unlock off for that station.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.20719.

## 0.1.3

- Each station's pots now change with what is planted: a pot in every bay for small plants, one big pot over two bays for a medium plant, and one over all four for a large plant. Corn, watermelon and other large crops grow in the middle of their pot instead of over the bay walls.
- The grow lights hang higher, so a fully grown crop fits under them: the tallest small crop in the Small, the tallest medium crop in the Medium, and corn in the Large.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.20719.

## 0.1.2

- With the Mod Manager, each station's `Materials` shows as item cards with a picker, and the station's package beside them with its pieces, weight and value.
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.20151.

## 0.1.1

- A change to `GrowthBoost` now reaches plants already growing, keeping the growth they have made.

## 0.1.0

- First build: a Small and a Medium Hydroponic Station, each with its own model and icon, and a new look for the game's Hydroponic Station (Large).
- Made for Survival Log 1.1.20151.
